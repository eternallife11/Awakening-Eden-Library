A little sacred. A little silly. Deeply devoted to life.

We’re Benjy & Sofia. We learn by putting our hands—and hearts—into what matters.

For more than a decade, our lives have been a living exploration of regeneration, organic living, permaculture, syntropy, agroforestry, yoga, holistic wellbeing and what it means to thrive in these times. These are practices we keep learning to embody—in our gardens, our bodies, our relationships and our everyday choices.

Across Australia, Colombia and Portugal, we’ve travelled between projects, volunteered, learned from generous teachers and immersed ourselves in the daily life of farms, retreats and communities. Along the way, we’ve helped manage retreat operations, facilitated gatherings, co-created events and food forests, and grown through challenging situations that asked us to listen more deeply.

There have also been nights sleeping beneath ancient rainforest canopies, barefoot walks in Australia’s tropical forests, encounters with magical cassowaries and birdsong that seemed to awaken ancient songs within us. Moments that reminded us how much we belong to the living world.

Awakening Eden is the meeting of these journeys—the lessons, gifts and longings of two souls woven into offerings for the wider family of life. Through practical guides, land work, yoga and the Living Library, we share what we’ve lived and what we’re still learning, while tending the seeds of future films, online gatherings and regenerative village life.

“A more beautiful future begins when inner care becomes visible in the way we restore land, grow food and belong to one another.”

Our shared vision

A living place where people, land and possibility can flourish.

Awakening Eden is a meeting place for practical regeneration, learning, community and the courage to build a more life-serving future together.

Healthy soil. Living water. Abundant food. Thriving ecosystems. Animals and wild places protected. Human beings remembering how to participate, not merely consume. We want to turn good intentions into gardens, food forests, restoration projects, gatherings, festivals, collaborations and places that genuinely increase the capacity of life.

Our mission

Regenerate life at every level.

Awakening Eden exists to protect life, restore relationship and help regenerate the living world — bringing back water, fertile soil, biodiversity, food, habitat and human belonging, from gardens and farms to watersheds, ecosystems and communities.

We work with regeneration, syntropy and symbiosis as practical guides: strengthening relationships, cycling resources wisely, increasing diversity, keeping living roots in the ground, caring for water and building systems that become more capable over time.

For us, interbeing is a guiding worldview. It reminds us that personal wellbeing, ecological health and community resilience are not isolated projects. We want to embody what we teach, speak up where life needs a voice, share useful solutions openly, and walk our talk with humility, humour and dirty hands.

That means co-creating regenerative land projects, workshops, circles, events, learning spaces, partnerships and, over time, regenerative villages and demonstration places where people can experience a different pattern of living rather than merely hearing about it.

We are especially calling in Earth guardians, stewards, practitioners, builders, educators, healers and fellow creators who feel the same pull: to build places where regeneration and wellbeing are embodied in daily life. Places that honour healing without becoming trapped in endless healing loops, and turn what we learn into how we grow food, care for water, build relationships, make decisions, create livelihoods and serve the wider living world.

Some people call this emerging way of living New Earth or 5D consciousness. We hold that language as a spiritual worldview and invitation: a shift toward greater awareness, responsibility, love, coherence and interconnection, expressed through our thoughts, emotions, inner work and, most importantly, the actions and systems we create together.

We also want to help redirect resources toward life: meeting aligned investors, philanthropists, land stewards and organisations now, while building regenerative livelihoods and enterprises that can allow us, in time, to become philanthropists and long-term stewards ourselves. The aim is not growth for its own sake, but greater capacity to protect ecosystems, restore water cycles, support animals and biodiversity, and seed projects that serve generations beyond us.

The future our hearts know is possible is not something we wait for. We practise it into being: inner, ecological, relational and economic regeneration made visible through the way we live, build, restore, gather, share resources and care for one another — together, as one family of life.

The gardens that grew us

From busy lives to a life organised around regeneration.

Our path has moved through Germany, Colombia, Australia and Portugal, through gardens, retreats, communities, land projects, mistakes, teachers and a lot of unlearning.

Across seven formative years in Australia, living systems and community spaces changed the way we listened: to land, to one another and to the relationships that actually sustain a good life.

In Portugal, the work has become increasingly practical: regenerative land design, water and soil thinking, food forests, orchard restoration, workshops, a growing Living Library and the long-term question of how regenerative places can support both ecology and human livelihoods.

Sofia’s reflection Regeneration is not only about planting trees. It is also about becoming more honest, more present and more able to live what we say matters.

Australia - learning to listen

Gratitude carried forward

For seven formative years, Australia changed the way we listened—to the land, to one another and to the deeper relationships that sustain life. We carry gratitude for the Country and communities that held this chapter of our journey.

Awakening Eden does not claim First Nations stories or symbols as its own. We honour living cultures with humility and believe true relationship begins with listening, respect, reciprocity and care.

With gratitude Benjy remembers Gabu Damien, a Warungu man from Far North Queensland, and the gardens and lessons in listening they created together. We thank Adam Collins, and Kate and Stu, our biodynamic teachers and wisdom keepers. And we hold special gratitude for Dan, Grace and Mark of the Sacred Earth Foundation family, whose vision and ours grew into a joined mission.
