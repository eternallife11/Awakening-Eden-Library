03 · Whole property · phased over years

Whole-Property Regenerative Design & Action Plan

For a whole quinta, farm, retreat or community project, where water, access, buildings, orchards, food production, habitat and future phases all have to work together rather than fight each other.

From€1,500Scoped after a site conversation

What’s included & how it works

How we read the land

  • Whole-property vision, needs and constraints
  • Site visit wherever practical
  • Pattern analysis: slope, sun, wind, water, vegetation, access, current use
  • Functional zones and how they relate to each other
  • Water movement, storage and drought resilience
  • Habitat, shade, biodiversity and fire-aware priorities

What you receive

  • A soil, biomass and fertility strategy
  • Orchard, food forest and productive area concepts
  • Access and workflow design
  • Site-suited plant palettes: what to plant together, where and for which role
  • Planting layouts, spacing, quantities and seasonal care schedules scoped to the agreed design brief
  • A phased implementation roadmap—what happens in year one, three, five
  • Concept drawings and an integrated property map
  • Two review conversations as the plan settles
  • Recommendations for specialists, contractors and collaborators

The aim: I can see how the whole property fits together, what belongs where, and how to develop it in sensible phases over several years.

This is a concept masterplan. It does not include licensed engineering, surveying, permitting or construction drawings. Where those are needed I will say so clearly and help you find the right professionals. Begins with a free 15–20 minute fit call.

A plan you can see, understand and use.

We turn the possibilities into a clear visual direction, planting relationships and phased actions you can actually use. The detail grows with the scope: from one focused area to a whole-property plan.

Luisa + Sim · Tábua, Oliveira do Hospital

Proposed food-forest gardens beside the levada

A food forest begins with relationships: existing trees, useful water, living soil and the people who will care for it. Here, the direction is a series of productive food-forest gardens, with an acid-loving berry row and a planted edge supplied by the levada.

The levada supply can be turned on and off. The proposed edge trench will be set out on contour after checking levels, soil, drainage and a safe overflow route. Plant choices and preparation for the berry row will follow the site’s actual growing conditions.

The next step is a detailed planting and action plan: what belongs together, where each plant goes, the support species and biomass needed, and a clear sequence from preparation to planting and ongoing care.

Meet the person behind the work

Hello, I’m Benjy.

I love helping people see what a piece of land could become—and find a first step they can actually take.

Working and learning across Australia, Colombia and Portugal has shaped my approach: observe carefully, work with living systems and keep learning from the place. Food forests, soil, trees and the people who care for them belong in the same conversation.

Sofia and I created Awakening Eden to connect practical regeneration with learning, wellbeing and community. My aim is to leave the land and the people caring for it more capable, connected and alive.
