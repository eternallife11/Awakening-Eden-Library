Rooted in lived experience, open to possibility.

A home is the beginning of a living place.

Years of living, working and learning in retreats, regenerative projects and communities have deepened our love for simple, beautiful homes surrounded by life.

We want to make that possibility more accessible: a shaded veranda, nourishing food close by, thoughtfully chosen materials, cared-for water and room to breathe. Whether you are creating your own sanctuary or exploring a shared project, we can help turn the dream into a grounded brief and a practical sequence.
