Truth protocol

Hope deserves rigorous roots.

Every entry should say what kind of knowing it represents: established evidence, a real project’s measured results, practitioner observation, traditional knowledge in context, emerging hypothesis, legal guidance or spiritual worldview. Attractive claims do not become facts through repetition.

Research map

Seven shelves before a site or offer.

  1. Land, tenure and stewardshipOwnership, community land trusts, cooperatives, leases, commons, succession, exit rights and protection against speculation.
  2. Affordability and housingReal build and operating costs, mixed-income access, financing, sweat equity safeguards, rental and ownership pathways.
  3. Water, food, ecology and fireCatchments, safe retention, soils, agroforestry, biodiversity, carrying capacity, drought, wildfire and long-term monitoring.
  4. Energy, materials and circular systemsPassive comfort, renewables, repair, low-impact materials, sanitation, nutrients, waste and appropriate technology.
  5. Governance, care and cultureDecision rights, conflict, safeguarding, children, elders, accessibility, privacy, rituals, burnout and belonging.
  6. Livelihoods and local valueWork that meets real needs without making residents dependent on tourism, unpaid labour or endless growth.
  7. Portugal feasibilityPlanning, land classifications, water and wastewater rules, fire obligations, licensing, tax, cooperative law and municipal relationships—checked against current official sources.
