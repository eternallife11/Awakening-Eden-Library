## Gallery behaviour and truthful visual language

- Desktop: one wide leading view and neighbouring cards, or about 2.3 cards visible. Phone: one comfortably large image plus a visible peek of the next. Manual swipe; desktop arrows; a simple position count or dots; a “View all” route. No autoplay. Keyboard arrows, visible focus, reduced-motion support and image alt text. Give concept visuals an always-visible badge. Provide a full-image view that does not crop teaching labels.
- Keep photo captions to a short category/place where useful. Put the details of intervention, Benjy/Sofia's role and project stage into a linked case study or accessible full view. A gallery without visible captions still needs accurate alt text and truthful context.
- Prepare responsive 640/1280 px derivatives from confirmed originals; strip metadata, set intrinsic dimensions and lazy-load later slides. The first visible image should have appropriate priority. Do not ship the source ZIP.
- A genuine “before/after” needs the same site and comparable views, dates and confirmed work. Existing site + a rendered proposal is **“Today / Design concept”**. Moinhos or Melides stage sequences can be **“Establishment in progress”** even when camera angles differ.
