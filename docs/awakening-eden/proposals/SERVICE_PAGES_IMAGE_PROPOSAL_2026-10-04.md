## What to send next — in priority order

1. **One wide whole-property photograph** from a high viewpoint: slope, trees, access and growing areas visible together. Landscape orientation, original resolution, soft daylight. A drone is optional, not necessary. A consented map is also useful, with exact private details withheld from public use.
2. **One current view of the documented planting project**, preferably taken from a previous camera position. Include the date, place and what changed. Do not stage a misleading before/after.
3. **One real teaching photograph** with Benjy or Sofia visibly explaining or demonstrating something. Supply the event/date, role, photographer and permission context.
4. **One example client deliverable**, anonymised: an actual plan, sketch, planting sequence or 90-day action page. This is the clearest way to explain the service value.
5. **One water-specific evidence image or 20–40 second clip:** rainfall moving through the site, a clearly explained water feature, or observations after rain. Say what it demonstrates and what is still an observation rather than measured improvement.
6. Optional: a high-resolution founders-at-work photo and one 20–40 second spoken invitation. The existing founder and Benjy portraits are already usable, so these are not blockers.

For each upload, a short note is enough: place/project, approximate date, who is shown, your role, permission to publish, and one factual sentence about the work. Avoid compressing originals through messaging apps if possible.
