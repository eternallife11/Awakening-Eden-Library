## 4. How a land walk works
We follow the water from high to low: where water arrives, where it runs and gathers, where soil is moving, how the soil feels, what is already thriving, and what people need (paths, buildings, tanks, drains, care capacity). Then we say what we saw, what still needs checking and the few steps that matter most. Field codes: E entry, F flow, P pooling, R erosion, S soil, A living assets, I infrastructure, O outlet.
