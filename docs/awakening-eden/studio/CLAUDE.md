## Truth rules
- Mark claims as established evidence, practitioner observation, emerging hypothesis, tradition or worldview.
- No healing claims for frequencies (432/528 Hz) or sound. No cure claims for back pain, posture or breathwork.
- Body and breath lessons: include a wellness disclaimer and contraindications. Flag back/core lessons for
  physiotherapist review.
- Name the teacher or source a technique comes from.

## Rights and policy
- Every image, clip and sound needs a recorded source and licence in the production's `metadata.md`.
- Prefer Benjy and Sofia's own footage and recordings. CC0 next. Never use unlicensed music.
- Label realistic AI-generated or altered visuals and voices (YouTube disclosure rule). Avoid mass-produced,
  repetitive uploads: each video needs a real human idea and real variety.
- Never show client property names, streets or coordinates without permission (see Fiona's Oasis handling).
