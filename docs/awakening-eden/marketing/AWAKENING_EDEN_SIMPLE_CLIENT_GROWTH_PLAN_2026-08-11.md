# Awakening Eden — Simple Client Growth Plan

**Date:** 11 August 2026  
**Focus:** heart-centred client acquisition in Central Portugal and online, without turning Awakening Eden into a content treadmill.

## The focus

For the next 30 days, market only two clear invitations:

1. **Regenerate Your Land with Benjy** — one focused pathway for quinta, orchard, garden and living-landscape owners. Online worldwide; on-site in Central Portugal.
2. **Journey with Sofia** — a gentle conversation for Yin, breath, embodied presence and slower, soulful movement. Begin privately or in a small group as availability takes shape.

Everything else—the free guides, Living Library, stories and community—is the trust ecosystem around these invitations.

## The simplest client journey

**Real story or useful teaching → one relevant page → one conversation → a human fit call → clear next step.**

Do not send paid traffic to the homepage. Send each person to the page matching the promise in the ad:

- Land ad → `/work-with-benjy`
- Embodiment ad → `/sofia`
- Warm community content → `/links`

Use one response promise everywhere: **“Tell us where you are and what you need. We will reply personally within two working days.”** Only publish that timing if it can be honoured.

## Organic rhythm: three useful pieces each week

- **One proof story:** a real garden, land observation, retreat lesson or honest before/after insight. Show people and place; say what changed and what was learned.
- **One practical gift:** a small action somebody can try today—soil cover, water observation, three slower breaths, a sensory walk.
- **One invitation:** name who the work is for, the felt problem, the outcome and one clear action: “Send LAND” or “Send BREATHE.”

Create once in vertical 9:16. Use the same core video on Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and Stories, then make a quieter square or portrait still for feeds. Keep text inside the safe central area and add captions. Meta’s current Reels guidance favours native 9:16 video with audio and key messages kept in the safe zone: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads

## Two repeatable content series

### Benjy — “One living-land observation”

Film 20–40 seconds outdoors:

1. Show the real place.
2. Name one pattern: bare soil, water leaving too fast, tired orchard, missing layers.
3. Share one generous insight.
4. Invite: “If you want a calm second pair of eyes on your land, send **LAND**.”

### Sofia — “One minute back in the body”

Film 20–40 seconds in soft natural light:

1. Offer one sensory cue or unhurried breath.
2. Name the permission: no perfect pose, no performance.
3. Let three real seconds of quiet remain.
4. Invite: “If you long for a slower practice, send **BREATHE**.”

Avoid medical promises. Describe felt experience, practice and support—not treatment or guaranteed outcomes.

## First paid test: conversations, not vanity metrics

Start only after the Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, WhatsApp Business number and reply routine are ready.

### Campaign A — Central Portugal land conversations

- **Objective:** Leads, with WhatsApp or Instagram Direct as the conversion location.
- **Location:** a realistic travel radius around your service area in Central Portugal; keep location as a strict control.
- **Creative:** three real variations: Benjy speaking on land; a field-work sequence; Benjy and Sofia planting together.
- **Message:** “Feeling overwhelmed by your quinta, orchard or garden? Get a clear, phased regenerative pathway grounded in your place.”
- **CTA:** Send message.
- **Qualification:** Where is the land? Approximate size? What is the biggest challenge? What would you love it to become?

Meta’s click-to-message ads can run across Facebook and Instagram and open Messenger, Instagram Direct or WhatsApp; Meta recommends conversation-focused optimisation when the goal is a conversation: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/click-to-message-ads

### Campaign B — online embodied-practice conversations

- **Objective:** Leads, initially through Instagram Direct or an instant form.
- **Audience:** adults in the countries and languages Sofia can genuinely serve; do not combine local land targeting with this offer.
- **Creative:** Sofia speaking, a slow sensory practice, and one real retreat reflection.
- **Message:** “Yoga does not have to be another performance. Come back to breath, senses and the body.”
- **CTA:** Send message or enquire.
- **Qualification:** What are you longing for? Private or small group? Time zone? What pace feels supportive?

Meta supports instant forms as a fast mobile-first option and website forms for higher-consideration services; its guidance also supports testing more than one lead route rather than assuming one will win: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-objectives/lead-generation/lead-ads-with-forms

## Budget and testing guardrails

- Begin with a **small 7-day learning budget you can comfortably lose**. Do not scale on likes, reach or cheap clicks.
- Put three creatives inside one campaign per offer. Keep the offer, destination and measurement consistent.
- Use Advantage+ placements for the first clean test so delivery can learn across Facebook, Instagram, Reels and Stories; Meta describes this as a way to find lower-cost placement opportunities: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/meta-advantage-plus/placements
- Review after enough real conversations have occurred—not after one day. Pause misleading or low-quality creative. Keep the message that creates qualified, caring conversations.
- Scale gradually only when real client value is higher than acquisition cost and delivery capacity remains healthy.

## The tiny scorecard that matters

Track weekly in one simple sheet:

| Offer | Spend | Conversations | Qualified conversations | Calls | Clients | Revenue | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Primary number: **cost per qualified conversation**.  
Secondary numbers: enquiry-to-call rate, call-to-client rate, revenue, and whether the work was a genuine fit.

## Privacy and measurement gate

The current website should not receive a Meta Pixel merely because “experts use one.” In Portugal/EU, tracking and advertising cookies require a properly designed consent and privacy setup. Decide the privacy approach first, then implement measurement deliberately.

If you later add measurement, use the Meta Pixel and Conversions API only with appropriate consent, data minimisation and policy review. Meta recommends using the Conversions API alongside the pixel for more reliable measurement, while explicitly noting that it is not a way around privacy controls: https://www.facebook.com/business/help/AboutConversionsAPI

Until then, use campaign-specific UTM links, message keywords (`LAND`, `BREATHE`) and the weekly scorecard.

## 30-day action plan

### Week 1 — make the invitation ready

- Confirm one reply owner and a two-working-day response routine.
- Connect Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and WhatsApp Business.
- Pin one Benjy invitation and one Sofia invitation.
- Record three vertical videos for each offer.
- Prepare saved replies that sound human, ask the four qualifying questions and never pressure.

### Week 2 — publish and listen

- Publish three organic pieces using the rhythm above.
- Personally invite past students, retreat contacts, land connections and trusted local partners to share the relevant page—without mass messaging.
- Join useful local conversations in Central Portugal groups by answering questions generously; do not drop links without context.

### Week 3 — run one clean test per offer

- Launch the two conversation campaigns with small, separate budgets.
- Check that every enquiry is answered, labelled and logged.
- Listen for the exact language people use to describe their needs; feed that language back into the page and creative.

### Week 4 — prune and deepen

- Keep the one or two creatives producing qualified conversations.
- Pause the rest.
- Ask every good-fit client how they found you and what made them trust you.
- Turn one real result or lesson into the next proof story, with permission.

## Heart-centred rules

- Invite; do not manufacture fear or inadequacy.
- Show real people, real land and real practice.
- Make ecological and wellbeing claims carefully.
- Never imply a future centre, land base or programme already exists.
- Protect rest and response capacity; a regenerative business cannot depend on burnout.
- Measure enough to learn, then return to relationship.
