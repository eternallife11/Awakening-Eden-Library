- Preserve the Tree + Heart identity.
- Preserve the Solar Tree–Heart–Lotus flagship portal and Rooted Human portal.
- The detailed portals are threshold art, not small logos.
- Use one dominant portal at most per page.
- Real photographs are the trust layer for people, land and completed projects.
- Painted botanical assets remain approved where the v3.2 register says RETAIN.

- Mobile meaning comes before explanation: shorten repeated intros, repeated CTAs and repeated imagery.
- Decorative art has empty alt text. Meaningful portal art has concise descriptive alt text.
