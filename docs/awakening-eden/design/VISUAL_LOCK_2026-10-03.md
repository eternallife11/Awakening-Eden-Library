## Style and placement

Blend botanical illustration with the strongest real images: parchment, olive/forest/teal, terracotta and honey; restrained rose/lavender; hand-drawn vines, water, roots, one hummingbird where relevant, and breathing room around type. The page should feel like a living illuminated field guide, with practical routes and trustworthy proof. Avoid a repeated wall of giant illustrations, false before/after pairings, dense ornaments or extra geometry.

The four doorway cards sit after the opening shared-vision text. The Library banner remains the Library's first major artwork and appears smaller on the homepage. The second circle illustration belongs beside Village Vision text, rather than displacing the brighter homepage Circle of Belonging.
