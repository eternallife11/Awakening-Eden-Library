# Asset Decision Register v3.2

## Retain as canonical

- `solar-tree-heart-lotus-portal-*`: flagship threshold; maximum once per page.
- `rooted-human-living-earth-portal-*`: Heart, Sofia, inner–outer regeneration and human-learning threshold.
- `flowing-suns-living-codes-divider-*`: strongest painted chapter divider; maximum once on a long page.
- `lotus-of-life-12-exact.svg`: geometry source of truth.
- `awakening-eden-mark-primary.svg`, `-reversed.svg`, `-one-colour.svg`: small brand uses.
- `painted-botanicals-approved/corners/*`: authentic photo and chapter accents.
- `painted-botanicals-approved/dividers/botanical-divider-*`: quiet editorial dividers.
- `painted-botanicals-approved/frames/*`: page-edge atmosphere on selected long guides only.
- `living-library-garden-book-*`: Living Library threshold; the responsive AVIF/WebP files are production assets.

## Retain but limit

- `painted-botanicals-approved/dividers/celtic-tree-of-life.webp`: heritage ceremonial divider only. It is not the master logo or the exact Lotus geometry. Use on at most one guide or Work-with-Benjy chapter after the proof section; do not repeat sitewide.
- Painted corners: no more than two visible corner motifs in one mobile viewport and no more than three motif families on one page.
- Page-edge frames: hide below 760 px unless the ornament remains outside reading content and does not create overflow.

## New v3.2 assets

- `links-moonlit-lotus-portal.svg`: compact, dark-page portal for `/links` only.
- `living-library-book-garden-portal.svg`: light vector fallback or small card version; do not replace the painted Living Library threshold where richness is wanted.
- `lotus-roots-botanical-divider.svg`: education, guides and regeneration transitions.
- `moon-seed-botanical-divider.svg`: Heart, Journal, Thriving and reflective transitions.
- `heart-hummingbird-vine-divider.svg`: About, Community and invitations.
- `photo-sprig-olive-rosemary.svg`: practical land/project photography.
- `photo-sprig-lavender-butterfly.svg`: people, ritual and reflective photography.
- `photo-root-fungi-water.svg`: wide lower edge on land, soil and water photographs.
- `root-water-page-edge.svg`: lightweight guide edge, used once and hidden on narrow phones.

## Replace

- Header, footer and favicon usages of `tree-heart-portal-01.webp` or `tree-heart-portal-02.webp` → the simplified vector mark family.
- Empty `rooted-human-living-earth-portal-1120.avif` from v3.1.1 → the repaired non-empty file in this package.
- Any public Flower-of-Life or Seed-of-Life graphic → exact Lotus asset or no geometry.
- Repeated 64 px Tree-heart icons between every section → one or two meaningful dividers per long page.
- Any stretched painted portal in the footer → one reversed vector mark.

## Retire from active use

- Old generated assets containing Flower of Life, Seed of Life or hexagonal circle lattices.
- Duplicate footer logos.
- Duplicate community photographs.
- Decorative botanical art behind body copy when it reduces contrast.
- PNG masters in production HTML/CSS.
- Multiple portal illustrations on one short page.
