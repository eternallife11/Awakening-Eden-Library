The visual system is guided by Awakening Eden's regenerative source of truth: relationship, succession, soil cover, living roots, water cycling, biodiversity, community and practical life-serving action.

The sacred-geometry asset is Awakening Eden's project-canonical twelve-petal Lotus of Life: twelve equal circles centred at 30-degree intervals on one orbit, each passing through a shared origin. It replaces every Seed-of-Life and Flower-of-Life construction in this visual system. The painted art may use poetic terms such as living lines, portals or earth frequencies. It must not claim to change DNA, heal disease or alter measurable frequencies without credible evidence.

Do not trace or reproduce identifiable VECA/Kathara diagrams, Indigenous or religious motifs, or the work of a living artist. The source is nature's observable repetition: circles, branching, spirals, networks, cycles, roots, water and growth.
