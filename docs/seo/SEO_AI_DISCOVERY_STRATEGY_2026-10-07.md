## Page ownership
- / — brand/entity + broad mission: Awakening Eden, regeneration, practical hope.
- /work-with-benjy — commercial land-design intent. Do not split into thin city doorway pages.
- /living-library — educational authority hub.
- /work-with-us — organizations, workshops and collaborations.
- /about + /journey — experience, identity, trust and provenance.
- /village-vision + /village-research — long-horizon community research and collaboration.

## AI / answer-engine discoverability
- Allow OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT Search discovery.
- Keep canonical pages indexable and directly accessible without login or JS-only rendering.
- Use stable Organization, Person, Service, WebSite and WebPage entities with consistent names and URLs.
- Write sections that answer real questions clearly in the first paragraph, then add depth, examples and evidence.
- Keep claims attributable. Link to primary sources where claims need evidence.
- Use descriptive headings, concise definitions, tables/checklists when they genuinely improve comprehension, and original images with descriptive alt text.
- Keep business/service areas consistent across visible copy, schema and external profiles.
- Earn mentions/links from real collaborators, farms, NGOs, municipalities, teachers and regenerative networks. Do not manufacture link schemes.
- Track Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance and grounding queries when available.

## Technical contract
Production must:
1. use https://awakeningeden.org as the canonical origin;
2. list only canonical production URLs in sitemap.xml;
3. expose sitemap.xml through robots.txt;
4. allow mainstream search crawlers and OAI-SearchBot;
5. keep flagship pages indexable;
6. give flagship pages one title, one meta description and one self-referencing canonical;
7. keep JSON-LD parseable;
8. prevent the retired Netlify origin from leaking into production text assets.
