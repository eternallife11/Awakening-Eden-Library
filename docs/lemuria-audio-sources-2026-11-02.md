All recordings are from Freesound and licensed **Creative Commons 0** (verified on each source page on 21 November 2026). CC0 needs no attribution; we credit every creator in the player anyway. The files are Freesound's high-quality MP3 previews, processed for the web (trimmed fades, loudness-matched, re-encoded). Lossless WAV masters need a Freesound login; the source pages below are where to get them.

## Considered and not used

- Flying over the hill HandPan (szegvari, 548026): melody AI-generated, then converted through a sample library.
- Handpan Performance (HECKFRICKER, 625312): audible air conditioning.
- Hawaii birds and soft rain (flio191, 269481): recordist describes the bird as annoying.
- SingingBowl.wav (Monkay, 48325), Tibetan singing bowl (enhuber, 400819) and Summer - Violin Clean (szegvari, 575619): previews peak at or above 0 dB (clipping).

## Synthesis

A soft synthesized bowl note on exactly 432 or 528 Hz (integer partials) and light synthesized chimes play in every soundscape; "Pure tones" plays only these. Rain and ocean are sound-designed filtered noise. No continuous drone. Nothing loads or plays until the visitor presses Play.

The soundscapes (Rainforest dawn, Hopeful sunrise, Empowering heart, New Earth, Pure tones) are listening moods, not health or therapeutic claims.
