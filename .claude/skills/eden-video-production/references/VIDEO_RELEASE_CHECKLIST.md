# Awakening Eden Video QA · release gate

## Story and truth
- [ ] Audience, purpose, correct platform, one valuable outcome and approved beat sheet.
- [ ] Every claimed site outcome is supported by actual footage/evidence. Concepts clearly marked as concepts.
- [ ] Actual Benjy/Sofia faces and voice identities preserved; no fake client testimony.
- [ ] Accurate captions, honest hook, no inflated outcome or misleading health/ecological claim.

## Visual
- [ ] Stage-approved storyboard matches rendered scenes; no unapproved changes.
- [ ] Intro, representative frames, transitions and ending visually inspected; no bad crop, jarring generated overlays or unreadable type.
- [ ] Current video style matches approved painted-field-journal or real field evidence; branding selective.
- [ ] Any animated map uses the same approved geometry, not invented hydrology.
- [ ] Motion comfortable, not generic unnecessary kinetic movement.

## Audio and legal
- [ ] Every audio/photo/video asset has owner/licence documented and permitted use.
- [ ] Audio played and checked for clipping, odd loops, sudden peaks, quiet dialogue, incorrect wildlife provenance and music balance.
- [ ] No copyrighted songs without licences; no stock pretending to be their site.
- [ ] Captions transcript accurate, synchronized, accessible.

## Render / publishing
- [ ] Actual file rendered and decodes; checked resolution, frame rate, duration, audio codec and container.
- [ ] Watch entire export or log precisely what could not be watched; playback on target phone marked human review if not performed.
- [ ] Source project and media traceable. Final thumbnail text/spelling checked.
- [ ] Client privacy approved and no platform publication without explicit consent.
- [ ] Benjy APPROVED the final version and any upload action separately.

**REJECT / STOP:** fabricated land result, changed identity, inaccurate captions or map overlays, unclear media rights, broken render, unapproved publication.
