The Bella 2 reality check

€30,595 is only the beginning.

The insulated BELLA 2 kit has an advertised starting sale price of €30,595. Pineca's published basic structure assembly is approximately €12,466. Before cooking a meal or taking a shower, we're already at roughly €43,061, excluding shipping, approved foundation, kitchens, bathroom, building services and utility connections.

That is why we budget €85k–€123k for a completed 60 m² project with a modest veranda allowance on a reasonably serviceable approved plot.

Typical extra cost buckets

  • Assembly and transport / unloading
  • Foundations, anchoring, drainage & roof finish
  • Kitchen, bath, hot water and finishes
  • Electrical, plumbing, heating and ventilation
  • Basic plot water, power and wastewater connections
  • Planning, architectural and engineering work
  • Veranda, shade, safe stairs and outdoor area
  • Contingency for surprises and material changes

For difficult off-grid sites, new drilling, long trenches, grid connections or extensive rockworks, add a separately quoted infrastructure budget.

05 · Site wisdom & next steps

Portugal-proof, not just brochure-beautiful.

Climate, natural materials and fire protection

Central Portugal can mean intense summer heat, winter humidity and frost, high autumn/winter rainfall, and wildfire exposure. Specify a real roof/wall/floor thermal calculation, vapour/moisture details, insect protection, cross-ventilation, shading and compliant fire-resilient detailing. “Natural pine” does not mean the entire kit is natural: many inexpensive models use polystyrene or polyurethane foam. A cork, wood-fibre or mineral-wool redesign may be possible with an engineer and supplier approval, but it will cost more.

Legal land permissions and septic / wastewater

Before buying kits, verify the plot's planning framework with the municipality, including PDM, permitted building footprint/usage, setbacks, access, fire constraints and water/wastewater treatment approvals. A moveable module, yurt, dome or prefabricated kit is not automatically exempt from construction or use controls. A wastewater system must suit soil infiltration and local water-protection requirements. Costs here assume ordinary site conditions, not a design approved by the municipality.

The supplier quote we should request

Ask each supplier for a line-item written offer: exact model and net internal area; engineering and performance data; roof covering; insulation materials/thickness and U-values; windows; floors; interior doors/partitions; foundation design; porch scope; delivery to Central Portugal; crane/unloading; assembly; waterproofing; kitchen; bathroom; electrical/plumbing; HVAC/ventilation; warranties; full price including applicable VAT; and whether the kit is documented for legal permanent residential occupancy.

What makes the homes regenerative?

Not the aesthetic alone. Prefer wood from responsibly managed forests, renewable insulation where viable, durability, water efficiency, breathable but airtight details, repairable components, passive solar positioning, ecological sanitation where lawful and suited to soil, shade trees and food forests designed with wildfire-conscious spacing. A stable home that lasts decades is more regenerative than a very cheap kit that rots or overheats.

Our first three test builds

For the first housing cluster, compare three real quotations against identical specs: OLIVIA (solo), ANGELA (couple + cats), and BELLA 2 (two bedrooms). In parallel, ask Green Heritage for an Essenza kit / full-home quote to test what a higher-natural-content house actually costs. Standardizing the main envelope and site infrastructure across multiple homes could reduce complexity, but savings need supplier evidence.
