Inner regeneration · practice

Breath, pranayama & embodied practice

Gentle foundations first, then traditional and teacher-led context. Please read the safety note before following any practice.

Before you follow any breath practice

These are educational resources, not treatment. Begin with breath awareness and comfortable, gentle breathing. Stop if you feel light-headed, anxious or unwell, and return to ordinary breathing. Rapid breathing, long retention and forceful locks are not beginner practices and are not for everyone.

Seek qualified individual guidance if you are pregnant, have a heart, lung, blood-pressure, eye, seizure or psychiatric condition, or if a practice provokes a strong reaction. Nothing here promises detoxification, trauma release or a guaranteed nervous-system outcome, and traditional explanations are not interchangeable with clinical evidence.

Regenerative livelihood

Money, sufficiency & regenerative abundance

What is wealth for, and does the way we create and use it leave life more alive?

Three different lenses live on this shelf, and they are not the same thing. Mindset and metaphysical books describe a worldview. Practical books describe money, time and choices. Regenerative-economics books make a values argument about what economies are for. None of them is individualised financial advice, and none guarantees an outcome. Quantum, neuroscience or universal-law language inside a popular book does not make its claims scientifically established.
